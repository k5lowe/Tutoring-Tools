Q: Find $f_{xx}$, $f_{yy}$ and $f_{xy}$ for $f(x, y) = {{coef(a,'x^3')}}y^2 {{term(b,'x')}}y^{ {{n}} }$.

Q: Solve $y' {{term(k,'y')}} = 0$ using an integrating factor.
A: $y = Ce^{ {{coef(-k,'x')}} }$
S: The equation is already linear in standard form $y' + P(x)y = Q(x)$ with $P = {{k}}$ and $Q = 0$. The integrating factor is $\mu = e^{\int P\,dx} = e^{ {{coef(k,'x')}} }$.
